Fair people-management path

A general guide in line with the ACAS Code of Practice. Always follow the company's own policy and involve HR for formal cases. Not legal advice.

  1. Informal conversation. Name the issue with facts, listen, agree support and a review date. Note it down.
  2. Investigate before deciding. Collect statements and records. Keep the investigator separate from the decision maker where possible.
  3. Invite in writing. State the allegations, enclose the evidence, give reasonable notice and the right to be accompanied.
  4. Hold the hearing. Let the colleague respond fully. Take notes. Adjourn if new evidence appears.
  5. Decide and confirm. Base the outcome on evidence, in proportion and consistent with similar cases. Confirm in writing.
  6. Appeal. Offer an appeal to a different, more senior manager.

Fairness check before any formal step

Absence and performance

Hold return-to-work conversations every time, look for patterns, ask what support helps, and set clear, measurable expectations with review dates. Check scheduling and pay are accurate before any conversation about attendance or hours.

Daily huddle and shift handover

Five-minute huddle

  1. Yesterday: one win and one miss, with numbers.
  2. Today: footfall, deliveries, promotions, absences.
  3. Customer focus: the one thing we fix today (queues, gaps, cleanliness).
  4. Safety: one hazard reminder.
  5. Recognition: name a colleague who did something great.
